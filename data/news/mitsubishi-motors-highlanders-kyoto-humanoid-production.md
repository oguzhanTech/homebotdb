**Mitsubishi Motors** and **Highlanders**, a **University of Tokyo** spinout, signed an **MOU** on **July 9, 2026** to jointly develop humanoid robots for Mitsubishi factories and to explore **mass production** of Highlanders machines at Mitsubishi's **Kyoto Plant**.

This is factory robotics, not a living-room companion. HomeBotRadar is covering it because automakers keep turning idle plants into humanoid lines, the same pattern we watch with **Figure 02**, **Unitree G1**, and Hyundai's Boston Dynamics path around **Spot**.

## What the deal covers

Mitsubishi and Highlanders will:

- Put Highlanders humanoids on **Mitsubishi manufacturing floors** first, to collect real operational data
- Study **mass production** of Highlanders products using **unused buildings** at the Kyoto plant
- Aim for production to become feasible in **early 2027**

Mitsubishi already invested in Highlanders and says it plans more. CEO **Takao Kato** framed the deal as building a foundation where humans and robots work together, and as a way for the carmaker to learn humanoid robotics as a business. Highlanders CEO **Hiroya Masuoka** called domestic mass production with an auto partner a step toward solving industrial labor gaps through physical AI.

Mitsubishi also calls this the **first** auto-maker plus humanoid-developer collaboration built around **mass production**.

## Scale and plant plans

Japanese reporting puts the ramp target near **1,000 units per month** once production matures, with idle **engine** line space in Kyoto reused for robot assembly. Treat that volume as a **reported ramp goal**. The companies' own wording sticks to early-2027 feasibility and unused Kyoto buildings, not a locked monthly quota.

## Factory first, then maybe other buyers

The first robots are expected to handle material moves, part placement, and other repetitive line tasks inside Mitsubishi. The partners may later sell to other manufacturers short on labor. Highlanders also lists general-purpose humanoids and quadrupeds plus simulators on its company profile. There is still **no consumer MSRP**, **no home ship date**, and **no retail cart**.
