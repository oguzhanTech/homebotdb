A track-built humanoid named **Tiangong Ultra** ran **100 meters in 8.86 seconds** in Beijing on **Tuesday, August 25, 2026**. That is faster than **Usain Bolt's** human world record of **9.58 seconds** from **2009**, and faster than the same robot's **9.39-second** heat on opening Saturday.

[The New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/08/24/world/asia/chinese-robot-100-meter-usain-bolt.html) used the Bolt comparison to ask what a sprint win actually means. For home buyers: not much yet. The robot is a **race specialist** from the **Beijing Humanoid Robot Innovation Center** (**X-Humanoid**). It is **not** joining our catalog.

BBC correspondent **Laura Bicker** filed from the Oval, including the 100-meter finish where sprinters cannot stop cleanly. The clip names Honor's **Lightning**. That unit ran **9.47 s** on Saturday, second to Tiangong Ultra.

[BBC News: humanoid 100-meter race at the Beijing games](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FGBLzMESBAo)

## What happened

- **Event:** 2nd World Humanoid Robot Games, **National Speed Skating Oval**, Beijing
- **Saturday, Aug 22:** Tiangong Ultra wins a large-size 100m heat in **9.39 s**. Honor's **Lightning** is second in **9.47 s**, also under Bolt
- **Tuesday, Aug 25:** Tiangong Ultra clocks **8.86 s** in a large-size 100m semifinal
- **Finish:** it hits a padded barrier, falls, and throws **sparks**. Crews carry units off. AP photos show staff putting out a fire

[Global Times](https://www.globaltimes.cn/page/202608/1368995.shtml) quotes the center on a lighter frame, stronger joints, and map-based navigation. The same briefing lists other claimed marks: **400 m in 38.15 s**, **1,500 m in 2:21.64**, and a **2.88 m** high jump. Treat those as company-timed event results, not a retail spec sheet.

## Why the Times is cautious

The Times talks to **Alan Fern** at **Oregon State**, who helped set a 2022 Guinness biped 100m mark of **24.73 seconds**. He calls the jump to **9.39** in four years a huge hardware-and-control gain, not a general-intelligence leap. He also notes many sprinters **cannot stop on their own**. They crash into pads.

**Michael Milford** at **Queensland University of Technology** told the Times that most robot sports do not map onto jobs people want at home. A staged 100m straightaway is not a kitchen. Fern's tennis point is the useful one: a Galbot unit rallying a human has to track a moving ball, which is closer to messy rooms than a dash. Galbot is still **not** in our catalog.

## What we do not know yet

Organizers have not published a **home SKU**, **MSRP**, or **retail region** for Tiangong Ultra. We also do not have independent timing lab notes beyond the Games clock and state-media recaps. Do not mix this with Unitree's earlier **Superman** peak-speed demo of **12.66 m/s**. That was a company sprint clip, not this 100m heat, and it is **not** the shipping **Unitree G1**.
