Figure AI says its BotQ plant in the US reached roughly **one humanoid robot per hour**. In about four months the line went from near one unit per day to that pace, a **24 times** jump. Figure described it as a demonstrated cycle for Figure 03, and said the line would keep being tuned.

## What Figure reported

- **350+** robot deliveries to date
- **500+** units shipped overall
- **55** robots planned this week alone
- **12,000** units per year as a stated capacity target

On the line itself, Figure added 50+ in-process checks and cites a first-pass yield above **80%**. Battery assembly is running at **99.3%** efficiency, and the team has produced **9,000+ actuators**. Each robot still passes 80+ functional tests, including a squat and a run, before it ships.

## Helix System 0 update

Figure also shipped a Helix System 0 update. Earlier builds leaned on proprioception; this version adds **stereo vision** so units can build a 3D read of the room. That is what backs the newer stair and uneven-floor demos on Figure 03 hardware.

## Figure 02 is off the line

The hourly pace in this story is the April report on Figure 03 at BotQ. It is not a claim that Figure 02 is still being built. On September 30, 2026, Figure said keeping the Figure 02 fleet no longer makes sense as the Figure 03 fleet grows. Figure lists that generation's firsts as the BMW deployment, the Helix software, a household-chore demo, and a logistics job. [Most of those units are gone](https://www.figure.ai/news/f-02-decommission). A few remain in storage at headquarters. Figure says the rest were shipped to a foundry in Imatra, Finland. That is the company's account of how it disposed of the fleet, not a new home product. There is still no home price for either generation.

Figure 02 is the retired generation on HomeBotRadar. This note does not add a Figure 03 row.
