**Gianna Capezio** paid **$30** for a one-hour humanoid clean in her **San Francisco** apartment on **Friday, August 21, 2026**. The robot made the bed, cleaned the bathroom, and vacuumed. She told [Storyful](https://www.usatoday.com/story/tech/2026/08/25/humanoid-robot-cleaning-san-francisco-home-video/91457398007/) it was slower than a person, but a **solid job**, and she did not redo the work.

Then she posted the clip. [USA Today](https://www.usatoday.com/story/tech/2026/08/25/humanoid-robot-cleaning-san-francisco-home-video/91457398007/) counted about **4.5 million** views in three days. [Moneywise, via Yahoo](https://ca.finance.yahoo.com/news/hire-human-san-francisco-woman-103000529.html) later put it above **5 million**. Commenters told her to **hire a human**. The robot was **not** working alone.

## What happened

- **Vendor:** **Tau Robotics**, San Francisco, invite-only
- **Price:** **$30** for one robot, one hour. Two robots in the same slot: **$60**
- **Date:** Aug 21 visit; viral clip in late August
- **Tasks shown:** bed, bathroom, vacuum
- **Control:** a trained operator plus AI, not full autonomy
- **Presence:** Tau's booking form requires an adult at home. Bathrooms and bedrooms are **off-limits unless you opt in**

[Tau's book page](https://www.tau-robotics.com/book) matches the $30 hour. CEO **Alex Koch** told [ABC7](https://abc7news.com/post/can-hire-humanoid-robots-tau-robotics-clean-home-san-francisco/19599847/) in July that current AI cannot fully run a humanoid in a house. A person drives it from a central site.

Koch told [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/offbeat/2026/07/29/big-vacuum-is-watching-as-robo-cleaners-put-humans-behind-the-wheel/5280674/) Tau designs cameras, claws, and onboard compute, and sources the rest of the body from **Unitree**. Treat that as a **modified fleet**, not the shipping **Unitree G1** in our catalog.

## Privacy is part of the product

Tau's [service privacy policy](https://www.tau-robotics.com/service-privacy) (July 27, 2026) is blunt: cameras record the visit to run the job and train models. [KTVU](https://www.ktvu.com/news/robot-house-cleaning-service-is-being-offered-bay-area-city) quotes the company saying that footage, including unblurred faces, is the training data. You can ask to delete raw files. Data already baked into a model is not pulled back out.

That is the $30 trade. You rent a teleop shift and feed a dataset. It is a different pitch from [Gatsby's $150 San Francisco clean](/news/gatsby-humanoid-first-us-apartment-cleaning), which claimed no human cleaner inside the unit. Tau wants you home, with a remote operator on the cameras.

## What we do not know yet

Tau has not published a spec sheet, autonomy rate, or independent timing of Capezio's hour. Koch told The Register the body is Unitree hardware. That is not a confirmed **Unitree G1** SKU on Tau's site. New foreign humanoid imports sit under our [FCC Covered List recap](/news/china-fcc-humanoid-robot-import-ban).
