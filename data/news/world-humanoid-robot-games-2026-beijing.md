The **2nd World Humanoid Robot Games** opened in **Beijing** on **Saturday, August 22, 2026**. [Anadolu Agency](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/science-technology/2nd-world-humanoid-robot-games-open-in-beijing/4034614) carried the **CGTN** count: **666 teams**, **2,056 robots**, and **16 countries** for a **five-day** meet at the **National Speed Skating Oval** (the Ice Ribbon). [Xinhua](https://english.news.cn/20260822/0aa7643b51d747dcacbac394a6e758f5/c.html) says the Saturday evening ceremony put about **1,000** humanoids on the floor.

This is a sports-and-work showcase, not a consumer launch. The home-robot angle is the scenario slate: organizers moved household, hotel, and factory tasks out of mock sets and into **model homes**, **hotels**, and **real workplaces**.

## What happened

- **Dates:** **August 22–26, 2026**
- **Venue:** National Speed Skating Oval, Beijing
- **Field:** 666 teams, 2,056 robots, 16 countries across six continents
- **Program:** **51** events and **1,301** matches across **nine** sessions
- **Split:** **30** competitive sports and **21** scenario-based challenges
- **Hosts:** Beijing municipal government, **China Media Group**, the World Robot Cooperation Organization, and the Asia-Pacific Robot World Cup International Council

[China Daily](https://www.chinadaily.com.cn/a/202608/14/WS6a7e8155a31073853ec534f7.html) reported the August 13 press conference. Teams are up **138 percent** from 2025. Robot entries have **doubled**. **China** accounts for **641** teams and **1,975** robots, from **157** companies plus **200** universities and research institutes. **Brazil** is sending a national team.

The 2025 inaugural meet ran **three days** with **six** sessions, **280** teams, and a little over **500** robots, per Xinhua and Anadolu Agency.

## Sports hall, then the house

New high-load sports this year include **long jump**, **weightlifting**, **tug-of-war**, and **table tennis**. Martial arts and dance are also on the card. Organizers say robots will often compete in **pairs or groups of four**, not only as solo acts.

The more useful signal for HomeBotRadar readers is the work block. Scenario events now cover **family services**, **hotel guest work**, **supermarket retail**, **industrial assembly**, **logistics**, and **firefighting**. Xinhua says those runs happen in real settings, not only simulated halls. That is closer to a home test than a 100-meter dash, even if the "home" is still a staged model apartment.

## The Unitree speed claim

Anadolu Agency also notes a **Unitree** humanoid that **reportedly** hit **12.66 m/s** (about **28.3 mph**), a tick above **Usain Bolt's** peak of **12.42 m/s** in his 2009 100-meter record. That figure is a **company sprint demo** for a research platform Unitree has been calling **Superman**. It is **not** a verified Games result, and it is **not** the shipping **Unitree G1**.

Treat **12.66 m/s** as an unverified peak until independent timing lands. Catalog G1 still lists about **7 km/h**. Different machine, different job.

## What we do not know yet

The opening recap does not name **winners**, **home SKUs**, or **retail prices**. We also do not have a public start list that maps every household-scenario entry to a product you can order. Autonomy versus teleoperation will vary by event. [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/johnkoetsier/2026/08/19/the-world-humanoid-robot-games-events-are-a-market-map/) and other previews have flagged that gap. Wait for results, not the parade.
