A customer shoved a humanoid named **Syoma** in an electronics shop in **Saratov, Russia**. The robot recoiled, dropped into a wide stance, and threw high kicks while staff tried to hold it. [Futurism](https://futurism.com/robots-and-machines/humanoid-robot-fights-back-shoved) recapped the viral CCTV on **Saturday, September 6, 2026**. One kick appears to catch a clerk. About 20 seconds later the machine freezes with its arms out.

The store joked that its "tech consultant" needed an **emotional reboot**. Nobody has shown a spec sheet. [Futurism](https://futurism.com/robots-and-machines/humanoid-robot-fights-back-shoved) and the [New York Post](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QuEsD-IyThM) say it **looks like** a Chinese **Unitree G1**. Treat that as a visual ID, not a Unitree press note.

[Saratov store CCTV: customer shove, then kicks (New York Post)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QuEsD-IyThM)

## What happened

- **Venue:** Volga Store 64, Saratov, Russia
- **Robot name:** Syoma, in-store "technology consultant"
- **Sequence:** handshake attempt, shove, fighting stance, kicks, staff restrain and power down
- **Injuries:** no serious injuries reported
- **Store line:** "lost control," customer left with a fright and "possibly a new iPhone"

[Yahoo](https://tech.yahoo.com/science/articles/man-pushes-humanoid-robot-gets-150951769.html) and [India Today](https://www.indiatoday.in/trending-news/story/russian-man-pushes-humanoid-robot-it-fights-back-in-terminator-style-moment-2986033-2026-09-03) match the store, the name, and the shutdown. Dates in those write-ups sit around **August 31 to September 2**. Futurism is the later English wrap.

## Hardware / context

A shipping **Unitree G1** is a compact developer humanoid, about **132 cm** and **$13,500** on Unitree's shop, often backordered. It is sold for labs and early adopters, not as a retail greeter with a kung-fu mode.

If this body is a G1 or a G1-class kit, that still does not tell you **who was driving it**. Futurism flags remote control, a canned demo, or a staged view-grab as live options. [Jam Press / What's The Jam](https://whatsthejam.com/world-news/humanoid-robot-attacks-customer/) is blunter: manufacturer, model, and control system are **not publicly identified**.

This is closer to [REK's San Francisco fighting-bot shop](/news/rek-san-francisco-humanoid-robot-fighting-storefront) than to a kitchen helper. Spectacle in a store aisle is not a home chore.

## What we do not know yet

Volga Store 64 has not published a model number, a Unitree invoice, or a safety log. We do not know if the kicks were autonomy, teleop, a canned motion, or a balance reflex that looked like a fight. We also do not have confirmed medical reports beyond "everyone is OK" from the store.

Do not mix this with Unitree's own dance or sprint demos. Those are company videos. This is store CCTV.
