Sony told Japanese owners on June 25, 2026 that aibo sales at home are winding down. The notice covers the ivory white ERS-1000. Stores sell what is left, then stop. The cloud plan, repairs, and the charging dock stay.

## The June 25 notice

The page is from Sony Group and Sony Marketing. It names one product: aibo, model ERS1000/W, ivory white. Domestic sales end once that stock is gone. Sony does not give a last calendar day.

What keeps running is listed in plain terms. The basic plan and the premium plan continue. So does care support, accessories, spare parts for the body, the My aibo app, repairs, and the aibo dock. Sony says it still wants aibo to stay a partner for people who already own one, and that it will add products and services later. The notice does not name a new puppy.

Owners can call aibo owner support on 0120-30-2629. Weekdays the line is open 9:00 to 18:00. Weekends and holidays it is open 9:00 to 17:00. The same page has an email form.

## The US store is still open

[Sony's US product page](https://electronics.sony.com/more/aibo/p/ers1000) still sells aibo at $3,199.99. That matches the catalog price. The price includes three years of the AI Cloud Plan. After that, renewal is $300 a year. The page says the sale is final, with no returns. Sony does not sell or ship aibo to Illinois or to Baltimore, Maryland. On November 10, 2026 the same page was still quoting a delivery window a few days out, so the US checkout was open.

A buyer in Japan should ask whether a unit is still in the remaining stock before paying. A buyer in the United States can still use the Sony store. HomeBotRadar still lists aibo as Buy Now in the United States and Japan. The Japan line stays until the stock is actually gone.
