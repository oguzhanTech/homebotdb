**Zhaopeng Chen**, founder and CEO of Munich's **Agile Robots**, told [Reuters](https://www.devdiscourse.com/article/international/3971394-german-robot-startup-agile-expects-humanoid-robot-market-to-overtake-auto-industry) on **Wednesday, September 2, 2026** that the humanoid market could grow to **10 times** the global auto industry **within years**. He argued every person, not every household, might someday have a mechanical companion.

"Instead of like one family has a car, I think every human being probably will be expected to have something like a humanoid to be accompanied with them," Chen said.

That is a CEO forecast, not a store page. [Roland Berger's 2026 humanoid study](https://mailing.rolandberger.com/hubfs/Roland_Berger_Humanoid_Robots_2026.pdf) is the better number sheet. It puts OEM-level humanoids at about **$300 billion by 2035** in a baseline case, up to **$750 billion** in an optimistic case, then up to **$2 trillion** (baseline) and **$4 trillion** (optimistic) **by 2050**. [StratNews](https://stratnewsglobal.com/technology/humanoid-robot-market/) and the Reuters wrap both cite the $4 trillion figure without that year. The year matters.

## What the sources actually say

- **Chen / Reuters:** one humanoid per person, market many times autos, "within years"
- **Roland Berger:** up to **$750B by 2035**, up to **$4T by 2050**, automotive-scale only in the long case
- **Agile Robots:** sales **€320 million ($370 million) in 2025**, aiming to **double** in 2026; about **$1.5 billion** raised, including **SoftBank** and **Xiaomi**
- **Bank of America**, via [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/investigations/chinas-humanoid-robots-arent-smart-enough-take-your-job-yet-2026-08-27/): about **20,000** humanoids shipped worldwide last year, **95%** from Chinese makers

Agile Robots is an **industrial** automation shop (arms, cobots, and the **Agile ONE** factory humanoid). [The Robot Report](https://www.therobotreport.com/agile-robots-launches-agile-one-industrial-humanoid/) covers Agile ONE as a plant coworker, not a living-room SKU. There is **no public home MSRP**.

Our June roundup of [Barclays' $200 billion by 2035](/news/humanoid-robots-trillion-dollar-market-outlook) sits in the same decade as Roland Berger's $300–750 billion OEM band. Chen's 10x-autos line sits far above both.

## Home angle

Chen's "every human" companion is the part shoppers will hear. Catalog reality is still **wave 1**: factories and labs. **Unitree G1** is the Unitree humanoid you can actually price, from **$13,500** on the official shop. **Figure 02** stays an enterprise prototype with no public MSRP. **NEO Gamma** is a US Early Access pre-order at **$20,000** (or **$499/mo**), not a mass appliance. **Apollo** is logistics-first.

Roland Berger also talks about **$2/hour** operating cost in plants and labor shortages pulling manufacturing back to Europe. That is a factory thesis. It does not put a humanoid in your kitchen this year.
