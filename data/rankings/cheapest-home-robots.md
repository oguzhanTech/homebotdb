mirumi is the lowest confirmed price, at $149.99. Vector 2.0 is $249.99, and the voice features need a paid plan. ElliQ shows $250, and that number is a lease start, not the whole cost. EMO is $279 and is sold as hardware.

## mirumi, Vector 2.0, and ElliQ

mirumi is a small companion, not a humanoid and not a pet that patrols the house. Vector 2.0 is Buy Now on anki.bot. The first 28 days of the voice plan have been included. After that, Hey Vector and ChatGPT need $11.99 a month or $99.99 a year. See it next to EMO on /compare/emo-companion-vs-vector-2. ElliQ's $250 is an initiation fee plus a monthly membership, hardware included while you pay, US only.

## EMO and the $399 group

EMO stays on a desk. Ropet KAMOMO is $349. Three robots tie at $399: Microduck as a pre-order, Misa, and Reachy Mini. Loona is $409 with no subscription for core play. Compare that pet with the desk robot on /compare/loona-vs-vector-2.

## Humanoids further down

Unitree G1 is $13,500. UWORLD U1 is $17,600+. NEO Gamma is $20,000. PM01 is $26,000. They are here because the price is confirmed and the status is Buy Now or Pre-order. Optimus Gen 2 has no store price, so it is absent. Atom's $27,500+ figure is an estimate, so it is absent too.
