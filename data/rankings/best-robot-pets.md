LOVOT leads the animal-like robots you can buy, at $3,850+. Loona is $409. aibo is $3,199.99. Vector 2.0 is here because people shop for it as a pet, even though it stays on a desk.

## LOVOT and Loona

LOVOT rolls slowly, asks for contact, and returns to a Nest dock. The robot page notes a monthly care plan besides the body price. Loona follows, plays, and docks itself, with no subscription for core play. The catalog price is $409. The KEYi page crosses out $529. A second US shop still lists $639. Compare that pet with the desk robot on /compare/loona-vs-vector-2.

## aibo, MarsCat, and Microduck

aibo includes three years of the cloud plan. After that, renewal is about $300 a year, and Sony does not ship to Illinois or Baltimore. MarsCat lists at $1,199 and is marked Buy Now. The two scores put aibo just ahead of it. The robot page says the store has shown the bundles sold out, so recheck the cart. Microduck, the $399 pre-order from the Reachy Mini team, sits just after MarsCat.

## Vector 2.0 and Spot

Vector 2.0 is $249.99. Voice, cloud, and ChatGPT need $11.99 a month or $99.99 a year. EMO is the other desk companion people compare it with, on /compare/emo-companion-vs-vector-2. Spot is a Limited enterprise quadruped, not a living-room pet. CORLEO is Coming Soon and has no price.
