LOVOT leads the robots you can order once humanoids are removed. Reachy Mini ties that score at $399+ and stays on a desk. EMO is $279. This is not a measurement of your hallway.

## LOVOT, Reachy Mini, and EMO

LOVOT is Buy Now at $3,850+, plus a monthly care plan on the robot page. Reachy Mini is the desk kit. EMO does not walk the house. If you want a pet that moves through rooms, Loona is $409 with no subscription for core play. Compare that pet with the desk robot on /compare/loona-vs-vector-2.

## Left off on purpose

Humanoids are off, including Unitree G1 and NEO Gamma. Stretch 4 is off because it is a tall helper at $29,950. Isaac 1 is off because the body rises to about 175 cm while it works, even though the base rolls. The full set you can order, including those three groups, is on /rankings/best-home-robots.

## A filter, not a floor plan

The cut is Buy Now or Pre-order, then everything except humanoids, Stretch 4, and Isaac 1. The order is the two scores added together. A high score does not promise the robot fits a studio. Pets are on /rankings/best-robot-pets. The catalog starts at /robots, and the Robot Matchmaker is at /wizard.
