Reachy Mini leads the desk robots with a confirmed price under $500. It is Buy Now at $399+. EMO is next at $279. Vector 2.0 has the lowest hardware price, at $249.99, before the voice plan.

## Reachy Mini and EMO

Reachy Mini is the Pollen Robotics desk kit. Microduck, the walking duck from the same team, is a $399 pre-order and is not a desktop robot, so it is off this list. See the pair on /compare/microduck-vs-reachy-mini. EMO chats and rolls on a flat surface. It does not walk the house.

## Ropet KAMOMO, ElliQ, and Vector 2.0

Ropet KAMOMO is $349. ElliQ's catalog price is $250, and the robot page describes a lease: an initiation fee plus a monthly membership. Vector 2.0 is $249.99. The first 28 days of the voice plan have been included. After that, Hey Vector and ChatGPT need a paid plan. Compare that desk robot with EMO on /compare/emo-companion-vs-vector-2.

## The $500 cap

The cut is desktop form, Buy Now or Pre-order, and a confirmed starting price under $500. The order inside that cut is the two scores added together. Romi is $658, so it is out.
