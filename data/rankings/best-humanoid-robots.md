Unitree G1 leads on the two scores added together, and it is Buy Now at $13,500. Reachy 2 is next and is Limited, around $70,000 for labs. NEO Gamma is the home pre-order at $20,000. Optimus Gen 2 is Coming Soon, and Figure 02 is Discontinued. Both stay on the table so the status is visible.

## Unitree G1 and NEO Gamma

Unitree G1 is about 132 cm tall, sold mainly for research and education, and the shop is often backordered. EDU pricing is quote-only. NEO Gamma early access is $20,000, or $499 a month, with a $200 deposit on the order page cited on the robot profile. It is a US pre-order. Compare them on /compare/neo-gamma-vs-unitree-g1.

## Reachy 2 and PM01

Reachy 2 is Limited and starts around $70,000 for labs. It ranks above NEO Gamma because reality is part of the total, not a tiebreaker. PM01 is Buy Now at $26,000. Treat it as a developer humanoid, not a family appliance.

## Optimus Gen 2 and Figure 02

Optimus Gen 2 has no list price and no reservation. The $20,000 to $30,000 figure is a target, not a store price. Figure 02 is Discontinued. Figure said on September 30, 2026 that most of that fleet is gone. See the gap against a robot you can buy on /compare/optimus-gen2-vs-unitree-g1.
