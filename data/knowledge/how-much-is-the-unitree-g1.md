Unitree G1 is the humanoid with a live base price. HomeBotRadar lists $13,500, confirmed. The product page and the UniStore shop are the two official doors. The shop cart is the reason the status is Buy Now rather than Pre-order. Units are frequently backordered, so limited availability is the honest second line. A backorder is still a cart. It is not a deposit on a robot that has no price.

## The $13,500 base

The base G1 is the standard model. Unitree's spec table puts the standing size near 132 cm tall, about 45 cm wide, and about 20 cm thick, at roughly 35 kg with the battery. The base joint count is 23. EDU versions run up to about 43 joints and add a development stack. Arm load is about 2 kg on the base model and about 3 kg on EDU. Battery life on the catalog sheet is about two hours. The $13,500 figure is the base robot before tax and shipping. EDU pricing is a sales quote, not a second sticker on the same page. Quote the EDU only after Unitree sends the number.

Hands are the usual place the total moves. A dexterous hand is an option on the higher trims, not a part you should assume is inside $13,500. If a configurator adds a hand, a battery, or a shipping lane, the checkout number is the one that matters that day. The catalog number stays the confirmed base until Unitree changes the shop price.

## The home humanoid next to it

NEO Gamma is the other humanoid shoppers put beside the G1. It is a US pre-order at $20,000 to own, or $499 a month, with a $200 refundable deposit. That is a home product with a 2026 delivery window. The G1 is a smaller research and education humanoid that you can try to buy now.
