PM01's official China shop lists ¥188,000. HomeBotRadar tracks that as $26,000, confirmed. Status is Buy Now, availability is limited, and the country on the row is China. If you can check out in yuan, the shop number is the one you pay. The dollar line is the catalog's conversion so the rest of the site can sort prices. It will not match a bank rate on the day you wire the money.

## The 2026 spec sheet

EngineAI describes PM01 as a lightweight, open humanoid for research, education, and demos. The 2026 education and navigation pages put it near 140 cm and about 44.5 kg with the battery, walking faster than 2 m/s, with a waist that turns past 300 degrees. Both trims list 24 joints, an 8-core CPU, a Jetson Orin NX, cameras at the head and waist, and about two hours on a 10,000 mAh pack. The navigation trim adds 3D LiDAR. Education keeps a chest screen and the secondary-dev access. An older FAQ on the same site once quoted ¥88,000. The buy page now shows ¥188,000. The catalog follows the current shop, not the old FAQ. Do not pay the old FAQ price.

Overseas buyers often meet a reseller quote instead of the China button. That quote can land in the same band and still is not the ¥188,000 button. Read the contract for warranty too. EngineAI has said the education trim carries a longer warranty than the commercial trim, and that the signed contract wins. A buy button in China is also not a promise of a US plug, a US manual, or a US service depot.
