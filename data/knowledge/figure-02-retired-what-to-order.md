You cannot order Figure 02. The catalog status is Discontinued, and the price is Unknown. Figure's September 30, 2026 note is the source for this answer. The company said keeping the F.02 fleet no longer made sense as the newer fleet grew. Most units are gone. A few remain in storage at headquarters. Storage is not a waitlist, and it is not a refurbished sale.

## September 30, 2026

Before that date, Figure 02 was already not a home product. It walked on two legs, used two hands, and showed up in workforce pilots, including a long run at BMW's Spartanburg plant. Those deployments were real, which is why the reality score did not fall to zero. They were not a checkout. There was no public list price before the retirement, and the decommission note did not add one. Older estimates that floated $18,000 or $20,000 were dropped from the catalog because no official price was ever published.

HomeBotRadar still keeps the profile so the retired generation can be compared with robots that have an order path. The status column is the point. A discontinued row is context. It is not an offer, and a higher score on an old demo does not bring the fleet back.

## NEO Gamma and Unitree G1

If you wanted a humanoid you can actually start, two catalog rows are the current paths.

NEO Gamma is the US home pre-order. Early access ownership is $20,000, or $499 a month, with a $200 refundable deposit. The order page still talks about 2026 US delivery. It does not promise a week. The side-by-side with the retired robot is /compare/figure-02-vs-neo-gamma. The dollar details live at /knowledge/neo-gamma-cost-and-ship-date.

Unitree G1 is Buy Now at $13,500. It is about 132 cm tall and sold mainly for research and education. The shop cart is live and often backordered. EDU pricing is quote-only. It is the clearest "add to cart" humanoid on the site, and it is a different kind of product from a home chore pre-order. Read both before you treat them as substitutes.

## Not a hidden Figure checkout

Figure has talked about a newer generation. That robot is not a HomeBotRadar row with a price and a status you can order against, so this answer does not send you there. Waiting on Figure 02 itself will not turn into a shipment. Optimus Gen 2 is also not the replacement: it is Coming Soon, with an estimate instead of a store price.
