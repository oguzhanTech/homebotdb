NEO Gamma is listed for one country. The catalog row says United States. The order page is the same story: early-access ownership at $20,000 is described with priority 2026 US delivery, and the $499 a month plan is a later delivery. Both are pre-order language. Neither is a tracking number. A friend in another country cannot use your US place in line.

## A window, then a factory note

The order page still says US deliveries start in 2026. It does not name a month for your address. An April factory note said units coming off the line were prioritized for internal home testing. That sentence matters more than a map. A robot in the company's own houses is not a robot on a US truck. Until 1X names a customer shipment, the status stays Pre-order and availability stays limited. Internal testing can run for months without a customer date attached.

A shopper outside the United States should not translate the page into a local pre-order. There is no public euro price, no UK date, and no second flag on the catalog row. If 1X opens another country, the robot page is where that list changes. The deposit holds a place in the US line. It does not buy a shipping address in another country, and it does not move the robot ahead of the company's own test homes. One US address per place in line is the offer as written.

## Unitree G1 ships more widely, and it is a different product

Unitree G1 is Buy Now at $13,500. Catalog countries are the United States, China, Japan, and Germany. The shop is often backordered, and a backorder is still not the NEO delivery window. The G1 is a smaller humanoid sold for research and education. NEO Gamma is the soft-covered home pre-order. Sharing a year on a calendar does not make them the same shipment.
