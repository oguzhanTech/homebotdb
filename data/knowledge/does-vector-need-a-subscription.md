Vector 2.0 costs $249.99 to take home. That price does not include the voice you see in the ads. The product page says Vector requires a paid subscription for voice commands, and that every new Vector includes the first 28 days free. Without the plan, the robot can still move and use Alexa. Hey Vector gets no answer, because that command is served from the Vector cloud.

## The plans on the membership page

The subscription page lists ChatGPT at $11.99 a month and $99.99 a year. The help center, updated September 21, 2026, adds the Claude backend at $14.99 a month or $139.99 a year. One membership covers one robot. You pick the backend in the Stratus account, enter the 8-character serial number, and pay through Stripe. Annual plans include priority support. The help center also says the first 30 days are free on a new order from anki.bot or Amazon, if you email support with the serial number and the order number. The product page says 28 days. Those two official sentences do not match. HomeBotRadar keeps both, and does not pick a winner.

Activation can take up to 24 hours, and longer if you powered Vector on before you bought the plan. The help center says a Clear User Data reset can skip that wait on a new robot. Firmware usually follows within 48 to 72 hours. Billing renews on its own. Annual customers get an email 72 hours before the charge.

## The robot without a plan

The help center is explicit about the unpaid robot. Alexa voice commands and autonomous behavior still work. Hey Vector and the AI connection do not. You still need a Stratus account to set the robot up, even with no membership. The product page puts the same idea in shorter words: without the subscription, Vector is not connected to the cloud, which is where the action happens.

The catalog price stays $249.99, confirmed, Buy Now. Open-box colors show up on the store at lower figures from time to time. Those are not the catalog price. Countries on the catalog row include the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, Germany, France, and Australia.

## Loona does not use this plan

Loona is the pet people compare with Vector 2.0. The catalog price is $439, confirmed, and the KEYi product page currently shows Loona Petbot Premium at $409 against a $529 list. Core pet play on Loona is not sold as a Vector-style membership. The gap is the point of /compare/loona-vs-vector-2. One robot is a desk companion with a cloud voice plan. The other is a wheeled pet with a camera, a dock, and a store price that moves with promos.
