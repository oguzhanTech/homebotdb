Doova is not for sale. HomeBotRadar marks it Coming Soon, with a price of Unknown and availability Coming Soon. Tuya Smart showed the robot at IFA 2026 in Berlin. The company posts describe a short wheeled companion with no arms, a 10.1-inch screen, and a face, built for older adults living alone. They do not include a dollar price, a deposit, or a month when a customer robot ships. No waitlist form is the same thing as no sale.

## The IFA 2026 description

The intended day is a home, not a factory aisle. If you call for help, Doova is supposed to find you and, if you do not answer, start a video call to family. It is also described as a chatter, a reader, a reminder for medicine, and a voice remote for Tuya lights and appliances. While you are out, it can roll a room and send a safety report. It docks itself. Whole-home search for lost objects is listed as a later add-on, not a feature on a shipping unit. Those features are a product story. They are not a SKU.

None of that is a cart. A trade-show robot with a help phrase is still a plan until a shop asks for money and names a date. The catalog has no country list yet, because there is no offer to attach a country to. IFA 2026 is the public debut, not a ship quarter. Until Tuya prints a price, Doova stays on the coming-soon list and off the buy list.

## ElliQ is the one you can start

ElliQ is Buy Now in the United States as a membership. The catalog price is $250. The membership page is a lease: $249 to start, then $59 a month, $588 for 12 months, or $936 for 24 months. You do not keep the device when the plan ends. That is a real offer with real limits. It is also the reason Doova and ElliQ belong on the same compare.
