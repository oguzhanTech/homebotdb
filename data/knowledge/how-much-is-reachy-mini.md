Reachy Mini is the open-source desk kit from Pollen Robotics, now part of Hugging Face. The catalog price is $399+, confirmed, Buy Now. The plus sign is there because two kits share the body and not the price. Lite is $399. It connects to a Mac, Linux, or Windows PC over USB, and the computer does the work. Wireless is $499. It adds a Raspberry Pi CM4, Wi-Fi, an IMU, and a 12.8 Wh battery so a demo can run without a laptop on the desk.

## The kit on the desk

You build the kit in about two hours. The head has six degrees of freedom, the body spins, and two antenna ears move. A wide camera and a four-microphone array face the room. Software includes a web dashboard, a simulator, ready-made behaviors, and Hugging Face Spaces apps. Hardware, firmware, and the SDK are open source under Apache 2.0. This is a robot you program. It is not a companion that arrives sealed and chats out of the box with no setup. Budget the afternoon, and a computer if you bought Lite.

Lead time is listed up to 90 days, and many orders ship sooner. The Pollen store ships to the European Union, the United Kingdom, the United States, and Canada. Other countries go through Seeed Studio. The catalog countries match that store list: United States, Canada, United Kingdom, and the European Union. A Seeed checkout can show a different total. The tracked prices stay $399 and $499. Neither kit includes a walking base. If you wanted a robot that crosses a room, this is the wrong row. The ears and the head are the whole body.

## Microduck is the other Pollen robot

Microduck is a 25 cm walking duck from the same company, with 15 motors and a grasping beak. The catalog price is $399, confirmed, and the status is Pre-order, aimed at Christmas 2026. Same dollar, different object. Reachy Mini is a head on a desk. Microduck is a small biped you wait for. The pair is /compare/microduck-vs-reachy-mini.
