You cannot buy Optimus Gen 2. The catalog status is Coming Soon. There is no reservation page, no deposit, and no country list. The price field shows $20,000 to $30,000 because that is the at-scale target Tesla has talked about. The price status is an estimate. It is not a shop price, and it will not hold a unit for you.

## No checkout

Tesla's AI page is the public home for the robot, and it still does not sell one. The October 21, 2026 shareholder update is the source for this answer. It says Optimus production has started at Fremont, on lines that used to build other vehicles, and that the ramp is early. California is the early site. Texas is still being built. The letter does not name a version, a unit count, a home ship date, or a dollar price.

That is why the catalog and the letter can both be true. Production for Tesla's own use is not the same thing as a product you can order. The first robots off a line can stay inside the company. HomeBotRadar does not turn a factory sentence into a cart.

## The $20,000 to $30,000 target

Treat the band as a goal for a later, larger run. It has shown up in Tesla comments for years. It has never shown up as a list price next to a buy button. Tax, shipping, a required plan, and which version you would get are all unpublished. If a store price appears, the robot page is the place it will change. Until then, quoting $20,000 as if you could pay it today is wrong.

Optimus Gen 2 is about 173 cm on the catalog sheet. Many of the other specs are still unconfirmed, because Tesla shares little outside demos and the shareholder letters. A height on a profile is not a delivery window.
