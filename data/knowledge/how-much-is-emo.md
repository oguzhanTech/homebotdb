## A desk, not a hallway

EMO chats, plays simple games, and can reach some smart-home devices through the Living AI app. It stays on a flat surface and rolls in small circles. It does not walk around a home, climb anything, or carry laundry. That limit is why it belongs on the desktop ranking rather than next to a humanoid. Battery, a specific country tax, and which color is in stock are store questions. The catalog countries are the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, and Germany. A white colorway and the standard unit can carry different store labels. Compare the unit, not the photo.

People shop EMO when they want a companion without a humanoid budget and without a pet that needs floor space. The price sits in the same band as other small robots you can cart this week.
