Loona is a wheeled pet from KEYi Tech. The catalog price is $439, confirmed. Status is Buy Now and available. The official Premium product page does not match that number today. It shows $409, with a $529 list crossed out, and it advertises a one-year warranty, support, and free shipping. The shop collection repeats $409 for Premium. Themed bundles on the same collection are higher. Buzzer Beater is listed at $468 against a $628 list, and Space Edition at $624 against $699. A separate US product URL has shown Premium near $639. Those are all KEYi pages, and they do not agree with each other.

## The catalog price stays $439

HomeBotRadar does not chase every promo. The tracked price stays $439 until an editor changes the row. This answer names the live page so you can see the gap. If you are about to pay, the checkout in your country is the number that matters. Tax, a bundle, and which storefront you opened can all move it. The $529 figure is a list price on the Premium page, not a second catalog sticker.

The robot itself is a small dog-shaped pet with a face, a camera, a depth sensor, and a dock it can return to. Chat features want Wi-Fi. Kids can program it with Blockly. KEYi says most of the sensing stays on the device. The catalog does not add a required monthly plan for the core pet, which is the split with Vector 2.0. Outfits, a second dock, and game props are optional and they are not inside the $439 line.

## Countries and the Vector comparison

Catalog countries for Loona include the United States, the United Kingdom, Germany, Canada, Australia, Singapore, and France. Shipping rules still belong to the store. A free-shipping line on a US page is not a promise for every country on that list. A collar, a ball, or an outfit can add a small second charge that never shows on the robot's price row.

Vector 2.0 is $249.99 for the hardware, then a voice membership if you want Hey Vector. Loona's promo price can land near Vector's hardware price, and the ongoing bill is the difference.
