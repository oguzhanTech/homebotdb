Isaac 1 has two home prices, and both are on the Weave Robotics product page. You can pay $7,999 up front, or $449 a month on the subscription. The order page asks for $250 today. That deposit reserves a place in line and is refundable any time until the robot ships. The catalog price is $7,999, confirmed. Status is Pre-order, and availability is limited. The monthly figure is the other official path. It is not a discount on the same checkout. Weave does not spell out the subscription length on the product page, so read the pre-order agreement before you treat $449 as a short rental.

## Who gets one first

The product page says first shipments begin in fall 2026. California comes first. Broader US availability follows through 2027. The catalog countries list is the United States. There is no European price on the page. After you pre-order, Weave says it will email you, and closer to delivery you can see the robot at a Weave location or on a video call. Commercial buyers are pointed to a separate contact. The home prices above are the home offer. A business quote is a different conversation and a different price.

Isaac 1 is a wheeled robot for laundry and resetting a room, not a biped. A remote operator can step in when it gets stuck. That is part of the product story on the company's own pages. A pre-order is still a pre-order. Fall 2026 is a season, not a week on a calendar you control. The deposit does not choose the payment plan. Weave says you settle buy-versus-subscribe as delivery gets close. Keep the agreement beside the $7,999 line so a monthly plan is not a surprise at the door.

## NEO Gamma is the other US pre-order

NEO Gamma is also a US pre-order, at $20,000 or $499 a month, with a $200 deposit and a 2026 delivery window. Isaac 1 is the wheeled home robot at $7,999. NEO Gamma is the humanoid.
