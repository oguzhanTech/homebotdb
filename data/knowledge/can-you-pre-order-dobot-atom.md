You can start a pre-order for Atom. You cannot treat it as a finished home product with one global sticker. HomeBotRadar lists the status as Pre-order and the price as $27,500+, and the price status is an estimate. The figure comes from China pre-sales that opened around March 2025 near ¥199,000. Dobot's own US and China pages are the places a local quote will show up. If those pages print a different dollar amount, the catalog keeps $27,500+ until the row is edited, and the official page is the one named here. Ask which Atom you are reserving before you pay a deposit.

## The full-size humanoid

Atom is Dobot's first full-size humanoid, from the arm maker in Shenzhen. The walking flagship, Atom Max, is about 165 cm and roughly 58 kg without the hands, with 41 degrees of freedom once both dexterous hands are counted. Arms are rated around 3.5 kg, with a higher short peak. Published runtime is about two hours, and a charge is about an hour. Dobot also shows Atom D, a shorter dual-arm torso for data collection. That torso is not the walking robot. A pre-order conversation should name which configuration you mean.

The pitch is work: assembly, café, and pharmacy-style demos, plus a straight-knee walk. A demo aisle is not a promise that the robot will run your kitchen on the day it arrives. Countries on the catalog row are China, the United States, and Germany. Availability is limited. A pre-order in one of those countries still has to name the trim, the hand set, and who trains the robot after it arrives. HomeBotRadar does not turn a trade-fair walk into a delivery week.
