ElliQ is the elder-care companion with a price that looks small until you read the lease. HomeBotRadar lists $250, confirmed. The membership page is more precise. A one-time lease initiation of $249 is billed today, together with the plan. Monthly is $59. Twelve months are $588, paid up front. Twenty-four months are $936, paid up front. The page presents the longer plans as $49 and $39 a month. Those are the prepaid totals divided by the months, not a bill that arrives every month at the lower rate.

## You lease the lamp

The membership page says the device is leased because ElliQ is an ongoing service. The fee covers the device, shipping and returns, setup help, software updates, a lifetime hardware warranty, the caregiver app, and support for as long as you stay a member. When the membership ends, the hardware goes back. The $249 initiation is not a down payment on a robot you keep. The catalog's $250 is the same class of number as that initiation fee, rounded to the tracked sticker. If the page and the catalog ever split by more than a dollar, the catalog keeps $250 until an editor changes the row, and this answer will still name the live membership figures.

Annual and 24-month plans include a 30-day risk-free trial. The monthly plan does not. The page also says you can move from monthly to a longer plan later. Shipping on the offer is US. The catalog countries list is the United States. A shopper outside the US should not treat the checkout as local. Call the number on the membership page if a state program or a health plan offers ElliQ at no retail cost. That path is not the $249 checkout, and it is not on the catalog price.
